# Calender and reminder app 

## Summary
This is an application on computer for students or anyone who need an easy and straightforward app to remind them of upcoming events or tasks.

## Problem it solves
This app is to help people to organize their schedule.

## Major features
* Add / Remove / Edit tasks
* Import from Canvas
* Notification through email
* Keyboard shortcut

## Technologies
* GUI: FTXUI/ Qt
* Language: C++
* Database: SqLite
* Data format: jSON
* Network: libcurl

## Intended Users
This app is for students who use Canvas and people who need a quick and easy way to plan their schedule.
