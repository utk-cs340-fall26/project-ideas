# Study Group Finder

## Description
This would be a web/mobile app where students can find or create study sessions for their specific courses. The goal is to make it easy for students to create study groups with classmates without having to know everybody's contact information in every class.

## The problem it solves
Sometimes it can be hard to put together a study group for a class. Whether it's the start of the semester and you dont know your classmates yet or its a Vol Core class outside of your major, not everybody knows who wants to study or how to contact them. The study group finder can help students form study groups with their classmates for all of their classes and lets them get quality studying done and meet their classmates.

## Major features list
- Students create an account, potentially verified with their school id.
- Students add all the classes they are taking to their profile from a selection of all UT classes.
- Students can create a study location for any class they are in or material they want to study.
- A feed will show upcoming public study sessions for all of the classes that a student is in.
- There can be notifications for when study sessions are created or you are invited to a study session.
- Attendance log so you can RSVP to study sessions to track the number of students attending.

## Technology stack
- Frontend in React.
- Database for storing all user info, maybe Supabase.

## Intended users and usecase
- All UT students would be the intended users.
- They would want to use this app because it makes managing study sessions easier. It allows them to better connect with classmates and get a higher level of understanding for their coursework. It also prevents the pain of having different GroupMe chats and group chats for every class on tons of different platforms.
