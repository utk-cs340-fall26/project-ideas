# Project Idea Proposal

## Project Idea

I propose a real-time collaborative music platform where groups can listen together synchronously with integrated chat. A user will be able to join a room, queue songs, chat, react to songs with emoji, and listen to music in real-time with their friends. Users will also be able to create rooms as a host, share invite links, and manage the song queue. Additionally, users in a room will have the ability to vote to prioritize a song in the queue. Songs in the queue will show who added them.

## Problem It Solves

The social features provided by the music streaming apps currently available on the market are decidedly lackluster. Apps like Spotify don't support real-time sync, feature UIs not suited for social interaction, and are often laggy which ruins the group-listening experience. An app such as the one I propose would offer a seamless way to listen to music in real-time with other people and chat about and react to it live.

## Major Features

- Solo playback
- Create and join rooms
- Collaborative queue
- Real-time chat

## Technologies

Frontend: React + TypeScript + Vite + Socket.io-client
Backend: Node.js + Express + Socket.io
Database: PostgreSQL + Prisma
Real-time: Socket.io
Music Data: YouTube Music API or Genius
Hosting: Railway or Render
Programming Languages: Typescript + HTML/CSS

## Intended Users

The intended users for this application would be anyone who wants to listen to music with friends or family without having to physically be in the same place as them or go through a third party like Discord. Apps like TikTok have proven that users want to listen to music together and, at the moment, there are no apps that specialize in this domain. As such, a music streaming app with a full suite of features dedicated to synchronous group-listening and real-time socialization will attract a significant userbase.
