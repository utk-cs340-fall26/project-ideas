### The Idea

An app for finding great places to skate, with user curated spots detailing the pros and cons of locations. Users can keep spots private, and use it as a private repository, or publish the spots they find.

### The problem it solves

When you move to a new city, or just start skating, you don't know any of the good places. Or, say you're just looking for something new? Cities don't always provide the type of information that would be useful to a skater, or if they do, it could be out of date. I speak from the perspective of someone who rollerskates, where smooth pavement and wide open spaces is a premium. When looking for greenways, for example, the city websites don't mention what the pavement is like, so I have to go there and physically try it out. I personally have been working on finding places that are good for rollerskating, but it would be nice to have an app that had this kind of info already. Plus, skateboarders, rollerbladers, scooterers may also want something like this.

### Major Features

*   User-made accounts
    
*   A database of locations, with images, user ratings, a brief description of the location, what activitie its best for, the rules of that location, etc etc.
    
*   A moderation system to ensure that locations provided are real, not dangerous, legal, and that information is accurate
    
*   Ability to bookmark favorite locations privately
    
*   Option to store places privately
    
*   User-created groups, where locations can be shared privately to just that group.
    

### Technologies

*   Map: MapBox or OSM
    
*   Database: Supabase
    
*   App framework: React Native (Javescript/typescript)
    

### Demographics

The intended users are people who rollerskate/rollerblade, and also possibly skateboarders but I'm not completely sure if they'd like this kind of thing. If you rollerskate/blade, you have specific interests that aren't really addressed by other apps, like pavement quality, cracks in the ground, crowded-ness, difficulty getting from your car to the trail in skates, if you're allowed to skate there, etc. This product would address these needs. I could also see it being used by skateboarders, as they especially have a big "spot" culture, though part of that culture is also exclusivity.
