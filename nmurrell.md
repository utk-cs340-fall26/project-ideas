#**Summary:**
I want to make a cutesy and casual to do list app that allows for a variety of task types while appealing to a casual audience who need additional motivators to get work done. 
**Problem:** Some to-do list style apps can be too overwhelming and rigid. Furthermore they can be unengaging and easy to drop. 
**My app’s solution:** This app is designed to create a more engaging system of completing tasks as well as providing a varied way to approach tasks. It is designed with a cute aesthetic to keep it from feeling overwhelming for users. It provides a more personal alternative to productivity apps for users in a way that aims to be fun and engaging. 
**Audience:** This app appeals to a casual audience who may struggle to be productive or find to-do lists too rigid. My app provides a fun and engaging way to stay motivated while also providing multiple ways to approach a variety of tasks.
##**Task Types:**
-Simple task items focusing on more mission based tasks such as: reply to an email, do laundry, etc. 
-Time focussed tasks, being something you estimate how long it will take and  creating time increments it will take to complete. 
-Projects, I want these to be large tasks you work on over time that you can set subgoals for to work on. 
-Future ideas, these are ideas that are not important right now but you can come back to later. 
##**Additional Features:** 
-Daily planning: users can plan for the day, adding tasks they wish to accomplish, looking at their projects, and adding goals to them if they see fit, 
-Point reward system that gives various points for completing tasks and planning the day. 
-Virtual office: Users can spend points to decorate their office and create a cozy environment. 
-Work mode:  Displays the office while users get to work on their tasks. This can act as a mental switch for the user saying “its time to work.” 
##**Tools:**
-Flutter: Main tool for formatting the frontend of the app. It has great potential to reach the desired look of my app and it handles well. 
-Dart: Main language used by flutter. 
-Supabase: Main tool to handle backend. Great database for storing user’s info, such as: account login, task states, and points.
