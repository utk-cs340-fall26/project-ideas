## A brief summary of the project idea:
Album music discovery and logging platform similar to Letterboxd. The user will have the ability to input an album they have recently listened to and receive three album suggestions using an AI-powered recommendation system. The three albums will have high, medium, and low levels of recognition tiers. Additionally, the user will be able to log their listening history, write reviews, assign star ratings, and save recommendations directly to their Spotify account.
## What problem it solves:
This platform would solve a few problems, mainly algorithm bubbles, lack of hyper personalize recommendations, and no dedicated album hub. Most modern music streaming platforms prioritize mainstream artists making it hard for listeners to branch out to smaller artists. Modern music streaming platforms recommendation algorithms don’t allow for controlled suggestions. While platforms like Letterboxd exist for film fans, there is a lack of the same type of platform for album based music fans.
## A list of major features:
+ Tiered 3-Album Recommendations (high/medium/low recognition) based on album input
+ Album logging with star ratings and reviews
+ Personal albums listening history and diary logs
+ Custom album lists (Ex: Fall vibes, 10 Top Favorites, Best of the 80s)
+ Spotify integration to open and save album recommendations on the users personal account
## Technologies you plan to use (languages, tools, third-partyAPIs/libraries):
+ **Frontend:** React, Next.js, Tailwind CSS
+ **Backend:** Node.js or Python (Node.js works best with Next.js)
+ **Database:** Supabase (PostgreSQL) 
+ **Third-Part APIs:** Spotify Web API, LLM API(Claude or OpenAI)
## Who the intended users are and why they would want this:
+ **Album Focused Listeners:** People who want to explore more albums with varying levels of recognition.
+ **Social Catalogers:** People who enjoy logging and rating music albums and creating and sharing lists of their favorite albums.
