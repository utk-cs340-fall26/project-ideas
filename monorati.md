## Summary
My project idea is for an app tailored for users to track their workouts in real time, structure their workouts through organization, and track their progressions for certain exercises. This app will also be tailored towards University of Tennessee Knoxville students, estimating how crowded the rec center is during certain times and based on the amount of students using the app at the rec center. 

## Problem
The problem that this idea would help solve is navigating the busyness of the rec center as it meets the needs of students. With a new influx of students and only one rec center for weightlifting, it can be frustrating to have a prolonged workout and no way to know when the rec center is crowded. Based on the busyness of the rec center, students would be able to either plan around the traffic or at least be prepared when they want to work out. Furthermore, students would also have something to track their workouts on and get good insight to the progress they are making in the gym.

## Features
Some of the major features that this app would include is all of the exercise tracking and structure it provides. Users would be able to track sets and reps for each respective exercise. They would be able to see previous workouts and what days they were active for each week. They would also be able to set goals and the app would track progress each time an exercise was done with more weight. The main feature that this app would provide is a way to see how busy the University of Tennessee’s rec center is at certain times of the day. This would be done through data based on presumed “busy times” which would get better over time. Users will have the ability to track whether they are at the rec center or working out somewhere else. If they are at the rec center, the amount of people logging a workout there would be used along with the time to determine how crowded the gym is.

## Technologies
The language used for this project could either be C++ or Python, but I am leaning towards Python to make it simpler to build overall. It would also use some API’s for exercise data and information to incorporate into the app. Finally, some AI tools can be used for the interface and workout summaries.

## Intended Users
The intended users for this project are University of Tennessee students who love the rec center. Knowing what times the rec center gym is busy can be very helpful for students who prefer to go when it is less crowded for a better workout experience. It also can be very helpful to students who want to track their progress over time. Students would be incentivized to both track their workout progress while also helping others reduce the traffic in the rec center gym.
