### Project Idea
- An application that aids people who want to make their own music.

### Problem
- Music theory can be a tough mountain to climb, so I would want this program to kind of help musicians or aspiring musicians navigation around that and create their own works seamlessly. Anyone who is new to an instrument typically does not have any formal music theory training and once a feel for the instrument is developed, they may begin diving into creating their own music. This can be a struggle though without prior knowledge of the music theory fundamentals. They may struggle to translate what they're feeling or thinking into actual structure or chords.

### Major Features
- Chord suggestion engine based on mood and/or genre
- In-browser playback
- Chord reader (if user wants to have the program analyze their own production)
- Alternate progressions to existing ones
- Creative direction assistance

### Technical Side
- Some music theory logic (Tonal.js)
- Audio playback (Tone.js)
- Github for version control
- VexFlow for chart illustrations

### Potential Users
- Musicians/songwriters
- Hobbyists
- Aspiring/new musicians
