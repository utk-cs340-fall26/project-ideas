The concept I have decided to propose relates to UT dining. I want to create a forum where students can look to see what is being served at each dining hall, as well as view information about the food.
Users will leave comments about the food and will be able to leave ratings. I want to create the vibe that students can leave comments without thinking about it too much. 
The idea is to create a social media-like forum where students can share thoughts and opinions, similar to Reddit. I also like the idea of up and downvoting comments to show the popularity of the opinion.
I also had the idea to incorporate student profiles where they can add dietary restrictions, food preferences, and have the app track what food they like. 
This way, the app can make suggestions based on their profile attributes. The app could contain some type of status that incentivizes users to keep leaving ratings and comments. 
The way I envisioned organizing the app was to have a section that contains each dining hall page. 
The user can then select the menu item from the respective dining hall and see reviews and comments about the items. 
I would also include another section containing the top-rated foods that are being served that day. A section could include the healthiest foods being served. 
The app will take food ratings and create an overall ranking for the dining hall. The dining hall page will also contain a space to leave comments about the environment, cleanliness, service, etc.
