# Digital Time Capsule

## Idea
The idea is for an application to store and release digital time capsules to friends and family. A software-defined way to put messages or other media into a digital format, and control when it is seen by the people you want to see it.

## Problem
Right now, if you wanted create a time capsule, you would create some physical box, put something in it, write when it is intended to be opened, and then hope somebody remembers or finds when the time comes. In the digital age, we should take advantage of the vast notification and distribution ecosystem that mobile phones create.

## Major Features
* Long-term capsule storage
* Recurring capsules
* Mass-capsules (send to the entire graduation class or friend group)
* Email delivery of capsule
* Mobile app notification of capsule

## Technologies
Frontend: React
Backend: Next.js or FastAPI (python)
Authentication: SupaBase or simple user/pass
Database: Postgres

## Intended Users
The intended users for this application would be anybody that wants to create a digitally-defined time-capsule for memories and treasures they want to share with their friends and family at a certain time.
