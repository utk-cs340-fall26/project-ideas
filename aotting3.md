# By students, for students! All-inclusive academic workload planner.

## Project Summary

My project involves an application for busy college students who want to find their perfect work-life balance. This application involves an assignment tracker, including assignment priority, class, assignment type, due date, and more. This tracker will be integrated with a calendar, that will include test and quiz dates, and more important student dates such as cancelled classes, or breaks. It will also help students to manage their time between assignments and also other student duties such as, providing group project tracking, semester planning, a reminder system, and a grade calculator.

## Problem
Lots of college students struggle to find a work-life balance, especially with a new & heavy workload. Often, students will lean too far towards one side, either slacking on grades, or not getting involved on campus. While college is for education, it's also an extremely important time to meet new people, get involved, and not only work hard, but play hard. This application will help students to integrate their assignments into their existing schedule, and still have free time. 

## Major Features

- **Syllabus reader**: This will allow a student to upload a class syllabus, and will automaticlly upload important dates into the integrated calendar. It will also create a class hub where the student can see key points from the syllabus: office hours, grade information, absence policy, and more.
- **To-do dashboard**: This is more than a typical student to-do list. Instead of the student having to manage their own decisions on when they will start assignments, this app can use AI to help the student figure out how early they need to start assignments, and this will refresh each day like "what should i work on tonight". It will include details about each assignment such as priority, due date, and difficulty.
- **Campus involvement hub:**  When being as busy as a college student, opportunities on campus can slip through your fingers easily. It will also include a campus events and applications hub that will encourage more involvement for students who use the app. 
- **Comprehensive Calendar**: Not your average calendar! Not only allows the student to log classes, appointments, meetings, etc, but also integrates optimal workload blocks. Has features tailored to students, such as exam countdowns, color coding, free time blocks, and could maybe even split into two calendars- academic and social.

## Intended Users
- Any college student, or more specifically, any student with a major that makes it feel difficult to have a good life balance. 
- College students with busy schedules, that may have to work during school, or do multiple extracurricular activities.
