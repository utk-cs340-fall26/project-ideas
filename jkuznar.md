# Resturaunt Picker

## Project Summary

This is an app that helps friends, family, coworkers and other small or large groups of people find a place to eat. Users would add resturaunts they want to dine at into a shared group and the app would give them a choice on how they want to decide where to go.

## The problem it solves

Have you ever gotten into an argument with family or friends on where to go to eat? Maybe you can never be decisive with where you want to eat. I personally speak from the perspective that I can never decide what I want. I sometimes will spend hours going back and fourth between resturaunts. From experience, most people are like this too. When you group these people together it makes it exponentially harder to actually get a decision, which can lead to conflict. This app would solve those issues and would make going out to eat more efficent and less stressful for all parties involed. 

## Major Features

* Users can create their own personal account with an email address
* Users can create, invite and join groups
* Users can add or delete restaurants they have already added to a group
* Users can veto a number of restaurants
* Groups can decide on how the app will assist in choosing a restaurant (random, vote, etc.)
* Groups can decide how many vetoes a user gets

## Technologies

* Framework: React
* Database: Supabase

## Intended Users

This app is intended for anyone that struggles coming to an agreement on where to eat with other people. The app is intended to make the decision easier and take the stress off of people, allowing them to just enjoy their meal. This will also help people avoid conflict over something so small with the people they are closest to. People already have enough stress in their daily lives, why add the potential for more?
