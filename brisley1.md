### Project Idea

The idea for this project is to develop a platform focused on music discovery, rating, and discussion. Users will be able to search for artists and albums, maintain a personal music library, rate and review music, and interact with other users.   

### The Problem it Solves

There are many platforms and apps that allow people to discuss, rate, and review media such as IMDb and Letterboxd, but there is a gap in this space when it comes to music. While music streaming platforms provide ways to listen and discover music, they generally don't focus on detailed reviews, ratings, and community discussion. This project intends to fill this gap by creating a dedicated platform where people interested in music can discover albums and artists, share their opinions, rate and review music, and interact with others who share similar interests.

### Major Features

* User accounts/profiles

* Music Search and discovery - search for artists, albums, and songs, browse by genre

* Rating and review system - allows users to give a rating out of 10 and a written review for any particular song or album

* Personal Music Library - users can build their library based on music they have listened to and plan to listen to

### Technologies

* Frontend: React + Typscript
* Backend: Python + FastAPI
* Database: PostgreSQL
* Music data: Spotify API or MusicBrainz API

### Intended Users

The intended users are anyone interested in discovering, discussing, and keeping track of music. This includes casual listeners who simply want to maintain a library of albums they have listened to or want to listen to, as well as more dedicated music enthusiasts who want to rate albums, write reviews, create lists, and discuss music with other users.
